Music engine

Star

Star is the studio’s music engine: the creative system behind the songs, the sound, and the film work — built to generate, shape, and finish music as a core studio discipline.

Sound genetics

MacSense

MacSense is built on sound genetics: every sound carries a Sound DNA genome of measurable traits — weight, shimmer, grit, groove. Parent sounds are bred into offspring with traceable lineage, and earlier projects and takes can be recovered rather than rebuilt. Ari, the built-in co-producer, proposes changes; the producer decides. Nothing is applied without approval.

MacSense sits inside the wider SIA V6 research program — the studio’s long-term work on its own AI systems.

Video

Savage Video Studio

Video tooling for short-form production: voiceover, b-roll, animation, and screen capture — a complete production pipeline built for a creator who works off-camera. Built for output, measured on what actually renders and posts.
