An AI operations platform under development, designed to discover business programs and partnerships, assist with applications, and track the work that follows.

Discovery

Programs, grants, credits, and partnerships a business qualifies for — surfaced and assessed instead of buried in portals and PDFs.

Prepared applications

Applications prepared from what the system already knows about the business, with a human decision before anything is filed.

Follow-through

Once an opportunity opens, the tasks, the deadlines, and the next steps are tracked until the work is closed.

Proof of done

Work is measured against evidence, not assertions: a task counts as finished when the result can be shown, not when it is reported.
