Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you submit the contact form, the studio receives what you type into it: your name, your email address, your company if you provide one, and your message. The site’s hosting provider also records standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited — to operate and secure the site.

Payments. Card payments are processed by Stripe through this site’s checkout links. Your payment details go to Stripe and are handled under Stripe’s privacy policy; the studio does not receive or store full card numbers.

How it is used. To reply to your inquiry, to deliver and support purchases, and to keep the studio’s business records. Nothing else.

No sale, no trackers. The studio does not sell personal information, and this site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Sharing. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to operate — hosting, email delivery, and payment processing — or when the law requires it.

Your choices. To ask what the studio holds about you, or to have it deleted, write to savagnic@gmail.com.

This site sets no advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. Any cookies present are limited to those essential to the site’s hosting and delivery, set by the hosting provider.
